# Shorts concept bank

Style rules (every video):
- Black void, glossy white 3D pictogram figures, warm beige bricks, mirror floor.
- **Two archetypes side by side**, labelled in small spaced caps (e.g. `MOTIVATED` / `CONSISTENT`).
- One idea per clip. The visual alone tells the story; text is the hook (first 2 s) and the payoff (last 5 s).
- 18–30 s, 9:16 (1080×1920), with a payoff that makes people rewatch or send it to someone.
- Keep text away from the bottom ~22% and right edge (Reels/Shorts UI covers it).
